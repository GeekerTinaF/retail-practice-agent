01

Executive intelligence

Q1

Current market state

Prestige beauty: contractingOnline share: rising from a low baseSpecialist retailers: stressed

Analysis French beauty demand is flat to negative in the selective channel, and the chains are under financial pressure. Prestige beauty fell −1% in value and −3% in units in 2025, while Europe grew +4%7. Douglas wrote down €99m of goodwill on NOCIBÉ10.

Q2

What materially changed

  • Jul–Sep 2026: CK Hutchison is in talks to sell Marionnaud (372 French stores) to the owner of Bogart. Unions struck on 5 Sep13.
  • May–Aug 2026: Douglas took the NOCIBÉ impairment, cut its guidance and reported France like-for-like sales of −4.3% in Q391011.
  • 1 Jul 2026: an EU €3 customs duty on low-value parcels took effect and replaced France's €2 fee2223.
Q3

Where fulfilment activity is concentrating

Analysis Fulfilment activity is concentrating in brand-owned direct-to-consumer (D2C) and e-commerce networks, not in retail chains: L'Oréal's pan-European e-commerce site at Bain-de-Bretagne (run by 3PL Dimolog), Aroma-Zone's new Cavaillon site, and Dior's new ID Logistics site in the Aube. Pierre Fabre cancelled a €70m greenfield DC and chose a retrofit instead.

Q4

Accounts with meaningful signals

No signal reaches High, because none has tier 1–2 evidence and a score of 10 or more.

  • Aroma-Zone Medium New 22,000 m² site; parcel volumes rising fast
  • L'Oréal / Dimolog Medium European e-commerce hub ramping; robots already installed
  • Dior · Marionnaud · Pierre Fabre · Shein/Temu Medium New 3PL DC, change of owner, DC retrofit, EU parcel duty
Q5

What Geek+ should monitor next

  • Cavaillon delivery and any automation vendor Aroma-Zone names (W02)
  • Further robot extensions at Dimolog for L'Oréal (W01)
  • The Marionnaud buyer's network plan after the six-month observation period (W03)
  • Automation tenders for Pierre Fabre's route de Seysses retrofit and Dior's Aube site (W05–W06)
Bottom line. Analysis In French beauty, the automation-relevant activity is in brand D2C and e-commerce fulfilment, not in the struggling perfumery chains. The next step is to confirm the evidence for a handful of named sites, not to run market-wide outreach.

Segment intelligence

At most three developments per segment. Evidence is Reported; "Why it matters" and "Logistics implication" are Analysis.

Selective / prestige retail (perfumery chains)

The selective channel is shrinking, and all its growth is online

What happened
Prestige beauty fell to €4.3bn (−1% value, −3% units) in 2025. Stores fell −4% while e-commerce grew +12%, taking the online share to 18%7.
Why it matters
Volume is moving from store replenishment to single-order parcel fulfilment.
Affected
Sephora, NOCIBÉ, Marionnaud; prestige brand houses
Logistics implication
More unit-picking and returns volume, and fewer store cases.

Two of the three national chains are under financial or ownership stress

What happened
Douglas impaired NOCIBÉ goodwill and cut its guidance1011. CK Hutchison is negotiating a sale of Marionnaud (372 stores, ≈€536m revenue in 2024) to the owner of Bogart13.
Why it matters
Store networks will shrink or be reorganised, and capex is likely to be constrained.
Affected
NOCIBÉ (Douglas), Marionnaud, Bogart group
Logistics implication
Possible network consolidation or a change of 3PL. New automated capacity is unlikely in the near term.

Sephora keeps gaining share at group level

What happened
LVMH Selective Retailing grew +5% organically in H1 2026. LVMH says Sephora gained "further market share … in many countries"12.
Why it matters
Sephora is the likely consolidator of share lost by weaker chains. France is not broken out.
Affected
Sephora (LVMH)
Logistics implication
There is no current evidence of new French DC capacity. The last verified site is Escrennes (FM Logistic, 2018)20.

Pharmacy & dermocosmetics

Pharmacy beauty is the growth driver of consumer health

What happened
The French OTC market reached ≈€17bn in 2025, with growth driven by beauty, hygiene and supplements. IQVIA forecasts +3–4% for 20268.
Why it matters
This is the one beauty channel with positive momentum and a very large number of delivery points (pharmacies).
Affected
Pierre Fabre, L'Oréal Dermatological Beauty, pharmacy wholesalers
Logistics implication
Many small, frequent pharmacy orders favour dense goods-to-person picking.

Pierre Fabre cancels a €70m greenfield DC and retrofits instead

What happened
A 73,000 m² platform planned for Muret (due to open in 2028) was cancelled in Sep 2025. The existing 36,000 m² site on route de Seysses will be modernised to absorb all Muret-basin logistics by 202817.
Why it matters
Capex was deferred and the project redirected to an existing building, where space constraints favour density.
Affected
Pierre Fabre
Logistics implication
A retrofit into an existing footprint suggests a need for storage density and phased deployment (Analysis).

Evidence gap: pharmacy e-commerce and wholesaler logistics

No tier 1–2 figure was retrieved for the online share of pharmacy dermocosmetics, or for 2025–26 DC investment by French pharmacy wholesalers. A search summary cited IQVIA at ≈3% online, but the source was not opened, so the figure is Unconfirmed and not used. See §9 G04.

Mass, digital-native & cross-border

Aroma-Zone is growing fast and adding capacity

What happened
Revenue grew +52% in 2025 (+56% in 2024). The company has 36 stores, with 15 more planned for 2026. It had 4.5m customers in 2025. It is building a 22,000 m² site in Cavaillon1516.
Why it matters
This is the clearest volume-growth signal in French beauty. An omnichannel mix (e-commerce ≈50% of revenue) is emerging.
Affected
Aroma-Zone
Logistics implication
A new site plus fast growth plus rising store replenishment points to demand for scalable picking (Analysis).

Cross-border parcels now carry an EU €3 duty

What happened
France applied a €2 fee per item category on non-EU parcels under €150 from 1 Mar 202623. It was superseded by the EU €3 duty from 1 Jul 202622.
Why it matters
It narrows the landed-cost gap between Shein/Temu and EU-stocked beauty sellers.
Affected
Shein, Temu, AliExpress; French mass beauty sellers
Logistics implication
It could push platforms towards EU-based stock, but no French beauty warehouse move has been reported.

Evidence gap: social commerce and mass-market (GMS) volumes

TikTok Shop (launched in France in 2025) and "dupe" retailers are named as disruptors7. The only size estimates found are agency blogs (tier 5), so none are used. No 2025 NielsenIQ hygiene-beauty figure for French supermarkets and hypermarkets (GMS) was opened. See §9 G05.

Fulfilment intelligence

Operational themes with evidence. Themes without verified French beauty evidence (returns, labour, peak) are listed in §9, not here.

Theme

Distribution centres
New sites: Aroma-Zone Cavaillon, 22,000 m² (Q3/end-2026)15; Dior / ID Logistics, Aube, 26,400 m², €45m, early 202718. Cancelled: Pierre Fabre Muret, 73,000 m²17.
New capacity comes from brands, not retailers. Capex discipline is visible, with retrofits preferred to greenfield builds.
E-commerce fulfilment
L'Oréal centralised its European e-commerce orders (24 brands, 14,000 SKUs, 100+ websites) at Dimolog, Bain-de-Bretagne: 1m orders rising to 1.7–2m a year14. Aroma-Zone ships ≈15,000 orders a day15.
This is a high-SKU, small-item, single-order profile, the classic goods-to-person case.
3PL relationships
Dimolog (L'Oréal e-commerce), ID Logistics (Dior), FM Logistic (Sephora Escrennes, 2018)141820. The NOCIBÉ–Viapost contract was last verified in 201621.
Beauty brands outsource heavily, so 3PLs are the decision point for automation in many sites.
Omnichannel
Douglas cross-channel services grew +18.2% in Q39. Aroma-Zone plans 15 store openings in 202616.
Sites must handle both store replenishment and D2C from shared stock.
SKU complexity
L'Oréal: 14,000 references at one site14. Aroma-Zone: 1,700 references15.
Long-tail slow movers drive storage-density needs. Dimolog uses its robots specifically for slow movers.
Automation
Dimolog runs a Scallog goods-to-person system (6 stations, 32 robots) integrated by AB Process14. L'Oréal's Vennecy DC (37,000 m², inaugurated in September 2022 or 2023, depending on the source) serves the Luxe and Consumer Products divisions19. Its automation scope was not verified.
Mobile-robot goods-to-person is already proven in French beauty e-commerce, so buyers will benchmark against it.
Inventory / cost
Cosmetics exports were flat, and US exports fell −19%6. Personal-care prices are rising below inflation2.
Margin pressure pushes cost per order to the top of the agenda.

Methodology

  • Produced with the retail-research skill V0.2: report-spec.md, evidence-policy.md, visualization-guidelines.md and commercial-intelligence-framework.md.

  • Conflicts show both values and name the more authoritative source; unresolved conflicts are carried to the watchlist.
  • Intelligence Priority scores are Analysis, based on the anchors in the framework. They are not a sales priority, and no CRM data was used.
