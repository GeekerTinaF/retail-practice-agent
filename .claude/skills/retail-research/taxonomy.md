# Taxonomy

Classify every finding by country, retail subcategory and news type.

## 1. Retail subcategory

Assign one primary subcategory. Note secondary subcategories where relevant.

- Grocery & Food Retail
- Fashion & Apparel
  - Report-level segments: **Apparel**, **Luxury Goods**, **Accessories & Jewellery**
- General Merchandise / Department Stores
- DIY & Home Improvement
- Electronics & Technology Retail
- Health & Beauty / Pharmacy
- Furniture & Home Goods
- Pure-Play E-commerce
- Third-Party Logistics (3PL) serving retail (including platform-run fulfilment such as ZEOS)
- Other / Cross-category (specify)

## 2. Country

Name the specific country first. Use the regional grouping only as a
secondary tag.

| Grouping | Countries |
|---|---|
| **DACH** | Germany, Austria, Switzerland |
| **UK & Ireland** | United Kingdom, Ireland |
| **Benelux** | Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg |
| **Nordics** | Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland |
| **Southern Europe** | France, Spain, Italy, Portugal |
| **CEE** | Poland, Czechia, and other Central/Eastern European markets |
| **Pan-European** | Use when a development spans groupings, and name the countries involved |

Comparison benchmarks (e.g. EU27) must be labelled as benchmarks.

## 3. News classification

An item may carry several classifications. Mark one as primary.

| Classification | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Expansion** | New stores, new markets, new distribution centres |
| **Automation & Technology** | WMS, robotics, automation investment |
| **M&A / Ownership change** | Acquisitions, mergers, sales of businesses |
| **Financial performance** | Earnings, guidance changes |
| **Leadership change** | C-suite, supply-chain or operations leadership |
| **Operational disruption** | Strikes, closures, insolvencies, supply-chain incidents |
| **Sustainability / Regulatory** | ESG commitments, compliance-driven change, customs and trade rules |
| **Partnership** | Vendor, logistics or technology partnerships |

## 4. Account role

Used in the Account Universe.

- Retailer (store-led)
- Retailer (online-led / pure-play)
- Brand (D2C + wholesale)
- Marketplace / platform
- 3PL / fulfilment provider
- Cross-border platform
